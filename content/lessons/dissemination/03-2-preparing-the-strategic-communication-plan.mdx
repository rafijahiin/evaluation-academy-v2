<Callout variant="key" title="Three commitments behind the plan">
**Transparency** — being open about the evaluation process, results, and limitations.
**Learning framing** — presenting the CPE as a valuable opportunity for growth and improvement, not a verdict.
**Trust** — credibility, quality, and ethical standards in the evaluation are prerequisites for trustworthy communication.
</Callout>

The CPE Manager and CO Communication Officer must also be **cognisant of the political environment** in which the evaluation was undertaken, the risks attached to communication activities, and ways to mitigate them.

<h3>Box 19 — Components of the strategic communication plan</h3>

<Checklist storageKey="ch5-l3-comms-plan" title="Box 19 — What must appear in the plan">
  <Item>**Communication objective(s)** — what the CPE communication is trying to achieve.</Item>
  <Item>**Target audience(s) for each objective** — who needs to hear what.</Item>
  <Item>**Evaluation products for each audience** — matching the audience-focused approach (lesson 5.2.1).</Item>
  <Item>**Dissemination modalities** — digital and in-person — with a clear timeline.</Item>
  <Item>**An adequately detailed budget** — to produce and disseminate each evaluation product, including copy-editing and translation costs.</Item>
</Checklist>

To ensure adequate resources are made available, the CPE Manager (with the CO Communication Officer) must include an **estimated communication budget in the overall CPE budget** in the costed evaluation plan — built at preparation phase. Budgeting communication after the fact doesn't work.

The final communication plan is **approved by the CO Representative** before implementation.

<h3>Accessibility and inclusion in the plan</h3>

The CPE Manager and CO Communication Officer should design accessible and inclusive communication approaches.

<KVGrid>
  <KV title="Reporting back to communities">Translating evaluation products into local languages; playing the evaluation video during CO field visits; presenting CPE results and the management response to communities; disseminating the brief in community gatherings.</KV>

  <KV title="Meaningful youth engagement">Engaging young people in developing the evaluation products — including input on the communication plan, communication products, channels, key messages, and dissemination modalities. Young people should also participate in long-term facilitation of use (lesson 5.5).</KV>

  <KV title="Digital accessibility (UNFPA 2020 guidelines)">Use accessibility features on the UNFPA website; make social media accessible; include international sign language in meetings and events; create easy-speak versions of technical materials; add closed captioning in videos.</KV>
</KVGrid>

<h3>The measurement framework</h3>

For each communication objective, the team prepares a measurement framework with indicators in two time horizons:

<KVGrid>
  <KV title="Short-term indicators">Audience reach and engagement — social media engagement; web visits at release; attendance at dissemination events; downloads of products; advocacy event metrics.</KV>

  <KV title="Long-term indicators">Utilisation of the CPE — references in policy documents; uptake in the next CPD; integration into joint programmes; citation in donor reports; references in technical decisions.</KV>
</KVGrid>

The UNFPA *Strategy to enhance evaluation use through communications and knowledge management 2022–2025* (used for centralized evaluations) provides a measurement framework template that can be adapted. The Communication Officer uses the data to inform the CO annual report and internal monitoring.

<h3>5.2.3 — Tools to develop the minimum package</h3>

The handbook lists the tools, templates, and guidance for each minimum-package product. The CO is not building from scratch — there's a UNFPA toolkit.

<KVGrid>
  <KV title="Evaluation report">InDesign template available (request from IEO at evaluation.office@unfpa.org). Editable Word and Adobe Illustrator templates for the cover. UNFPA IEO supplementary editorial guidelines.</KV>

  <KV title="Executive summary">InDesign template available. Guidelines and structure for a CPE executive summary published by the IEO.</KV>

  <KV title="One-page brief">Illustrator template available. Guidelines and structure for a CPE brief. Also: ESS/DEVCO How-to guide on evaluation briefs.</KV>

  <KV title="Infographics">Canva or Lucidchart. ESS/DEVCO How-to guide on evaluation infographics.</KV>

  <KV title="Videos">Laptop camera, Zoom, mobile phones for recording. Adobe Premiere Pro, Canva, or DaVinci Resolve for editing. IEO and ESS/DEVCO guides on remote and standard video recording.</KV>

  <KV title="Social media (X / formerly Twitter)">Canva for visuals. Hashtags from IEO examples: #PopulationEval, #UNreformEval, #YouthEval.</KV>

  <KV title="Newsflash emails and web articles">CO external mailing tools; UNFPA IEO supplementary editorial guidance for web articles.</KV>

  <KV title="Presentations">CPE presentation template (with guidelines on slide 1).</KV>

  <KV title="Photos for products">UNFPA multimedia library; UNFPA multimedia consent forms (lesson 3.2.5).</KV>

  <KV title="M&E Net community blogs and podcasts">ESS/DEVCO how-to guides on evaluation blogs and evaluation podcasts.</KV>
</KVGrid>

<InPractice country="The accessible communication test">
The handbook flags a specific accessibility test: can a community member with limited formal education access and understand the CPE results in their local language? If the answer is no, the CO has produced a report for itself — not for the rights-holders whose lives the country programme is meant to change. The brief in local languages, the video during field visits, the community presentation — these are the proof of accessibility in action.
</InPractice>

<KeyTerms>
  <Term name="Strategic communication plan template">UNFPA-provided template for structuring the plan around the five strategic elements and Box 19 components.</Term>
  <Term name="Costed evaluation plan">The overall CPE budget submitted with the CPD, which must include a ring-fenced communication budget line.</Term>
  <Term name="Measurement framework">A set of indicators (short-term: reach and engagement; long-term: utilisation) tracked across the dissemination phase and beyond.</Term>
  <Term name="Digital content accessibility">The UNFPA 2020 guidelines covering website features, social media, sign language, easy-speak content, video captioning.</Term>
</KeyTerms>
