<Callout variant="key" title="What the management response does">
The management response presents the recommendations of the CPE alongside the CO's responses to them. **Reflecting on and formulating responses to the recommendations is a crucial step to foster ownership** and ensure timely, effective use of evaluation findings — to address weaknesses and inform the next programming cycle. Through the process, CO staff and stakeholders review each recommendation and agree the actions to implement it.
</Callout>

<h3>The six-week clock</h3>

The management response must be **completed within six weeks** of the communication of the final CPE report to the IEO. Under the CO senior management's leadership and the CPE Manager's guidance, the response requires contributions from many staff across the CO.

<h3>The management response workshop</h3>

The CO Representative calls relevant CO staff and **select stakeholders** (government partners, implementing partners, development partners, representatives of rights-holders organisations) to a CPE management response workshop.

Participants consider each recommendation and **first determine whether it is**:

<KVGrid>
  <KV title="Fully accepted">The recommendation should be completely implemented. The response indicates how, by whom, and by when action will be taken.</KV>

  <KV title="Partially accepted">Some part of the recommendation is not realistic or suitable. The CO must explain why the partial — and indicate what action will be taken on the remaining parts.</KV>

  <KV title="Not accepted">The recommendation does not logically derive from the conclusion(s) of the report, AND/OR calls for actions not within the control of intended users. The reason for rejecting must be explained.</KV>
</KVGrid>

<Callout variant="warn" title="The four positions — and the missing one">
The handbook lists **three** valid positions: fully accepted, partially accepted, not accepted. There is no "defer indefinitely" option. Every recommendation gets an explicit position with an explanation. This is what makes the management response auditable.
</Callout>

<h3>Drafting responsibilities</h3>

Once consensus emerges on which recommendations are accepted, partially accepted, or rejected:

<KVGrid>
  <KV title="CO Representative allocates drafting">Each accepted (or partially accepted) recommendation is assigned to a relevant staff member — e.g. the CO maternal health officer drafts the response to a maternal-health recommendation.</KV>

  <KV title="Responsible staff consult">When drafting responses, they consult concerned parties — government partners, implementing partners — as appropriate.</KV>

  <KV title="Actions must be practical">Actions in response to recommendations must be practical and implementable, technically sound, consistent with ongoing and planned developments in the country programme, and suited to the country's evolving context.</KV>

  <KV title="Action timelines">Actions should ideally be scheduled for implementation **within a year or two** of finalising the management response — the purpose is to improve the next country programme, not track routine actions common to all country programmes.</KV>
</KVGrid>

<h3>CPE Manager's coordination role</h3>

<Checklist storageKey="ch5-l4-mgmt-response" title="CPE Manager — management response coordination">
  <Item>Ensure all CO staff submit draft responses by the set deadline.</Item>
  <Item>Compile all responses into the management response document, following the UNFPA Guide on development, reporting, and tracking of management responses (specifically section 3 on elements of a good management response).</Item>
  <Item>In consultation with the RO M&E Adviser, perform an in-depth QA of the draft management response.</Item>
  <Item>Submit to the RO M&E Adviser for review and final approval.</Item>
  <Item>Send the approved management response to the Programme Division's Strategic Planning Branch for upload to **TeamCentral** (UNFPA's management response-tracking database).</Item>
</Checklist>

<h3>The relationship with the IEO quality assessment</h3>

The management response process is **separate** from the IEO's evaluation quality assessment of the final CPE report. But there's an interaction:

<Callout variant="warn" title="If the CPE is rated unsatisfactory">
If the IEO rates the CPE as "unsatisfactory", the CO may want to **reassess its proposed actions** in light of the level of confidence that should be placed in some findings, conclusions, and recommendations. In that case, the CPE Manager seeks guidance from the RO M&E Adviser and the IEO, then advises the CO Representative.
</Callout>

<h3>Tracking implementation</h3>

Under the CO Representative's authority, the CPE Manager reports on management response implementation **in TeamCentral at least twice a year until all actions are implemented**.

<KVGrid>
  <KV title="Programme Division (PD) Strategic Planning Branch">Tracks completion of management responses and reports to the UNFPA Executive Committee and Executive Board on the use and follow-up of evaluations.</KV>

  <KV title="Closure of recommendation actions">The PD is responsible for closing completed recommendation actions, in consultation with the CPE Manager, under the CO Representative's authority.</KV>

  <KV title="Management response closure">Closed as soon as fully implemented — or no later than **5 years after the evaluation year**.</KV>

  <KV title="Audit risk">The management response and actions taken (or not) can be subject to audit by the UNFPA Office of Audit and Investigation Services (OAIS) and/or the Board of Auditors.</KV>
</KVGrid>

<h3>Figure 8 — Management response process at a glance</h3>

<KVGrid>
  <KV title="Step 1 — Kickoff">CPE Manager and CO Communication Officer kick-start the dissemination and facilitation of use phase.</KV>

  <KV title="Step 2 — Parallel tracks">**Track A:** Management response workshop distributes drafting tasks; CO staff prepare their contributions; CPE Manager consolidates. **Track B:** RO M&E Adviser sends the final CPE report to the IEO; IEO performs the EQA.</KV>

  <KV title="Step 3 — QA and approval">RO M&E Adviser supports the CPE Manager in QA of the draft management response. CO Representative reviews and approves.</KV>

  <KV title="Step 4 — Submission">CPE Manager submits the final management response to the Programme Division and IEO.</KV>

  <KV title="Step 5 — Upload and tracking">PD uploads the management response to TeamCentral; tracks implementation; closes actions when completed.</KV>

  <KV title="Step 6 — Reporting">CPE Manager reports on implementation in TeamCentral at least twice a year; uploads to CO website.</KV>

  <KV title="Step 7 — Publication">Management response, CPE report, and EQA all uploaded to the UNFPA evaluation database.</KV>

  <KV title="Step 8 — Audit pathway">The management response remains subject to audit by OAIS and/or the Board of Auditors.</KV>
</KVGrid>

<h3>Publication</h3>

The CPE Manager and CO Communication Officer ensure the **management response is published along with the CPE report and the EQA** on the CO website. The IEO ensures the CPE report, management response, and EQA are publicly available in the **UNFPA evaluation database**.

<InPractice country="The transparency model">
Publishing the management response alongside the report — together with the IEO's quality assessment — is what makes the system honest. Stakeholders can see what was recommended, what the CO agreed to, what was rejected (with reasons), and what the IEO thought of the evidence behind it all. The full pipeline is in public view.
</InPractice>

<KeyTerms>
  <Term name="Management response">The CO's documented response to each CPE recommendation — accepted, partially accepted, or not accepted — with action, owner, and timeline.</Term>
  <Term name="TeamCentral">UNFPA's management response-tracking database, managed by the Programme Division's Strategic Planning Branch.</Term>
  <Term name="EQA">Evaluation Quality Assessment — the IEO's rating of the CPE report's quality, published alongside the report and the management response.</Term>
  <Term name="PSIPB">Programme Division's Strategic Planning Branch (also referenced as "PD"). Tracks management response completion and reports to the Executive Committee and Board.</Term>
  <Term name="OAIS">UNFPA Office of Audit and Investigation Services — the management response can be subject to audit by OAIS and/or the UN Board of Auditors.</Term>
</KeyTerms>
