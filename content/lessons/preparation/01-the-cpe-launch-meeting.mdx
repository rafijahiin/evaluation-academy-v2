<Callout variant="key" title="The 11-month minimum">
The handbook is explicit: allow at least **11 months end-to-end** from the launch meeting to the final report, so unforeseen delays don't push the CPE past the deadline for informing the next programming cycle.
</Callout>

The **CO Representative nominates the CPE Manager.** Where the post exists, the CO M&E Officer acts as CPE Manager. Where it doesn't, the CPE Manager is a CO staff member designated by the Representative in consultation with the RO M&E Adviser.

The CO Representative gathers all CO staff and the RO M&E Adviser in a launch meeting. The CPE Manager presents the **CPE Roadmap** — the milestones diagram that takes participants through the five phases (preparation, design, fieldwork, reporting, dissemination & facilitation of use). The roadmap helps everyone visualise the process, identify its key milestones, and determine what needs to be done, who needs to do it, and how and when.

<h3>What gets distributed at the launch</h3>

During the preparation phase, shared tasks include:

<KVGrid>
  <KV title="ToR contributions">Programme, operations and communication staff each contribute to specific sections of the CPE ToR and annexes.</KV>

  <KV title="Document repository">Collection of background documents and filing them into the online document repository (lesson 1.7).</KV>

  <KV title="ERG members">Identification of country programme stakeholders who should take part in the Evaluation Reference Group (lesson 1.5).</KV>

  <KV title="EQ workshop participants">Identification of who attends the evaluation questions workshop (lesson 1.3).</KV>

  <KV title="Selection panel">Formation of the panel that will select the consultants (lesson 1.6).</KV>

  <KV title="Time schedule">The CO Representative, with the CPE Manager and RO M&E Adviser, sets a realistic time schedule reflecting all activities at each phase.</KV>
</KVGrid>

The time schedule must ensure the CPE report is ready in time to inform the preparation of the subsequent programming cycle. The CO should allow enough time (**11 months minimum**), so that if unforeseen delays occur, it is still possible to meet the final deadline.

After the launch of the CPE, the government partner or CO Representative (on behalf of the government partner) circulates an official letter to inform all key stakeholders about the commencement of the CPE.

<InPractice country="From the handbook">
Box 1 of the handbook spells out exactly what the launch meeting must accomplish — a seven-item checklist the CPE Manager works through before adjourning the room.
</InPractice>

<Checklist storageKey="ch1-l1-launch-meeting" title="CPE launch meeting checklist (Box 1)">
  <Item>The Ready-to-Use (R2U) ToR template is shared with and presented to all CO staff.</Item>
  <Item>The role and responsibilities of CO staff members are established at each phase; there is clarity on who needs to contribute what to the development of the ToR and annexes (including document repository) and by when.</Item>
  <Item>The role of the ERG is explained and its membership is discussed and agreed.</Item>
  <Item>The overall composition and profile of the external evaluation team is agreed (and the option of contracting a consultancy firm vs hiring individual consultants is examined).</Item>
  <Item>The composition of the panel to select the consultants is set out.</Item>
  <Item>The CPE timeline is fixed.</Item>
  <Item>The list of participants and a date for the evaluation questions workshop are announced.</Item>
</Checklist>

<KeyTerms>
  <Term name="CPE Manager">CO M&E Officer (or designated staff member) who manages the day-to-day operations of the CPE without compromising the evaluation team's independent judgement.</Term>
  <Term name="CPE Roadmap">Milestones diagram showing all five phases with their deliverables, used at the launch to align all CO staff.</Term>
  <Term name="R2U ToR">"Ready-to-Use" Terms of Reference template provided by the IEO. Reduces drafting time and standardises the CPE structure across countries.</Term>
  <Term name="RO M&E Adviser">Regional Office Monitoring & Evaluation Adviser who provides oversight and quality assurance throughout the CPE.</Term>
</KeyTerms>
