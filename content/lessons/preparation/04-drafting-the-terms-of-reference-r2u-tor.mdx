<Callout variant="key" title="The ToR as the contractual framework">
The ToR defines the objectives and scope of the evaluation; spells out the evaluation criteria and preliminary questions; presents a tentative time frame and work plan; articulates the qualifications and roles of the consultants. **As the basis for the contractual arrangements with the evaluation consultants**, clear, comprehensive and well-written ToR are crucial for a smooth CPE.
</Callout>

The CPE Manager must use the **R2U ToR template** to draft the CPE ToR. The template provides fully drafted sections that require minimal adaptation — so the country office can focus on the most critical part: developing context-specific evaluation questions through a participatory process. It also reduces lead time in drafting, review and approval, helping the CO recruit an evaluation team in time so the CPE can start as planned.

<h3>The two-stage drafting process</h3>

The development of the ToR is a **team effort** under the leadership of CO senior management and the guidance of the CPE Manager. Many CO staff contribute. The ToR is developed in two stages:

<KVGrid>
  <KV title="Stage 1 — ToR main body + annexes A & D">
    The CPE Manager uses the R2U template to draft the main body of the ToR (including section 14: Bibliography and Resources), with contributions of relevant CO staff and QA by the regional M&E adviser. Once approved by the RO, the CO publishes the call for evaluation consultancy with the final ToR plus annexes A (methodological annex) and D (work plan).
  </KV>

  <KV title="Stage 2 — annexes B & C">
    As the call is published and consultant selection proceeds, the CPE Manager (with contributions from CO staff) completes the remaining annexes: stakeholder map (annex B, see lesson 1.9) and the list of UNFPA interventions (annex C, see lesson 1.8). Upon recruitment, the CPE Manager shares all annexes with the team — they are crucial for the design report.
  </KV>
</KVGrid>

<h3>Why the two-stage approach</h3>

<KVGrid>
  <KV title="Confidentiality">Annexes B and C may contain confidential information; sharing them only with the selected evaluation team protects sensitive material from being exposed during the call for consultancy.</KV>

  <KV title="Time">The CPE Manager gets more time to seek input from CO staff and complete the stakeholder map and interventions catalogue properly.</KV>

  <KV title="Parallel progress">The often-lengthy process for pre-selecting, pre-qualifying, selecting and recruiting consultants can run in parallel with annex completion.</KV>
</KVGrid>

<h3>The flow at a glance (Figure 3 in the handbook)</h3>

<Checklist storageKey="ch1-l4-tor-flow" title="ToR drafting process (Figure 3)">
  <Item>Launch meeting distributes tasks; CO staff prepare their respective contributions to ToR sections.</Item>
  <Item>Evaluation questions workshop generates and prioritises 6–8 questions.</Item>
  <Item>CPE Manager drafts the ToR with annexes A and D using the R2U template.</Item>
  <Item>ERG reviews the draft ToR and provides comments.</Item>
  <Item>CPE Manager consolidates the draft ToR.</Item>
  <Item>RO M&E Adviser reviews and approves.</Item>
  <Item>CO Representative reviews and approves the final ToR.</Item>
  <Item>CO procurement unit publishes the call for evaluation consultancy with annexes A and D only.</Item>
  <Item>In parallel: CO staff prepare contributions to annexes B (stakeholder map) and C (interventions catalogue).</Item>
  <Item>CPE Manager prepares annexes B and C; RO M&E Adviser reviews and approves.</Item>
  <Item>Upon consultant recruitment: CPE Manager provides final ToR and all annexes to the consultants; procurement attaches them to the consultants' contracts.</Item>
</Checklist>

<InPractice country="From the handbook">
A common mistake is to delay the call for consultancy until annexes B and C are perfect. The two-stage approach exists so the call can go out as soon as the main ToR is approved — typically reducing total CPE timeline by several weeks.
</InPractice>

<KeyTerms>
  <Term name="R2U ToR">"Ready-to-Use" Terms of Reference template provided by the IEO. Fully drafted sections that require minimal adaptation, plus four annex templates (A, B, C, D).</Term>
  <Term name="Annex A">Standard methodological annex — describes the CPE methodology in detail. Published with the call.</Term>
  <Term name="Annex B">Stakeholder map (lesson 1.9). Completed in Stage 2; shared with the selected team only.</Term>
  <Term name="Annex C">List of UNFPA interventions for the period under evaluation, including budgets and expenditures (lesson 1.8). Completed in Stage 2.</Term>
  <Term name="Annex D">Work plan and timeline. Published with the call.</Term>
  <Term name="IEO">UNFPA Independent Evaluation Office — provides oversight on the ToR and the wider CPE.</Term>
</KeyTerms>
