<Callout variant="key" title="Why the repository matters">
It is **crucial** to identify the core documentation required for the CPE ahead of time and ensure it is readily available to the evaluators — via an Online Document Repository — as soon as they begin the assignment. Without a comprehensive set of well-organised and easily accessible documents, the evaluators will not be able to undertake the documentary review necessary to design the CPE.
</Callout>

<h3>Who builds it</h3>

The **CPE Manager**, with the support of the **YEE** and in collaboration with **relevant CO staff**, sets up and populates the repository. The handbook provides a **document repository checklist** in the CPE Management Kit. The checklist:

<KVGrid>
  <KV title="Folder structure">Defines the standard folder organisation for every CPE — so consultants moving between country offices find a consistent layout.</KV>

  <KV title="Key documents per folder">Lists the documents that must appear in each folder — leaving no ambiguity about what is "required" vs "nice to have".</KV>

  <KV title="Filing status tracking">Tracks which documents are filed and which are outstanding, with a status column.</KV>

  <KV title="Responsibility assignment">Indicates the person or unit responsible for uploading each document — programme officers for thematic reports, operations for AWPs, communications for partner reports, etc.</KV>
</KVGrid>

<h3>What goes in</h3>

<KVGrid>
  <KV title="Strategic and programming documents">Country Programme Document (CPD), the country programme's theory of change and integrated results and resources framework.</KV>

  <KV title="Annual planning and reporting">CO Annual Work Plans (AWPs — original and revised versions); implementing partners' AWPs; UN joint programmes and joint work plans; Country Office Annual Reports (COARs).</KV>

  <KV title="Previous evaluations">Earlier CPE reports; project evaluations; mid-term reviews; research studies.</KV>

  <KV title="Project documentation">Project documents; progress and monitoring reports; field mission reports (often a wealth of action points).</KV>

  <KV title="Partner and donor reports">Implementing partner work plan progress reports; donor reports; joint programme reports.</KV>

  <KV title="Technical and statistical">Technical studies and reports; statistical reports; audit reports.</KV>

  <KV title="National surveys">Demographic and Health Surveys (DHS); Multiple Indicator Cluster Surveys (MICS); national censuses; other major national household surveys.</KV>

  <KV title="UNFPA centralized evaluations">Independent Evaluation Office centralized evaluations germane to the country programme thematic scope (the IEO publishes the complete list).</KV>
</KVGrid>

<h3>Connection to the design phase</h3>

At the design phase, **extensive document review forms the bulk of the evaluators' work**. The repository is the team's first and most-used resource. As they move forward in the design (and later the field) phase, evaluators complement the initial batch with documents they identify or that CO staff bring to their attention.

<InPractice country="What “good” looks like">
A well-run country office maintains the document repository **continuously across the programme cycle** — not as a one-off task during CPE preparation. When the CPE arrives, most of the work is already done. Repository hygiene becomes a habit, not a scramble.
</InPractice>

<Checklist storageKey="ch1-l7-repository-readiness" title="Repository readiness checklist">
  <Item>Online Document Repository created with the standard folder structure from the CPE Management Kit checklist.</Item>
  <Item>CPD, strategic plan, and theory of change uploaded.</Item>
  <Item>All AWPs (CO and implementing partners) for the evaluation period uploaded — original and revised.</Item>
  <Item>All COARs for the evaluation period uploaded.</Item>
  <Item>Earlier evaluations (CPE, project, MTR) and research studies uploaded.</Item>
  <Item>DHS, MICS, census and other major national surveys uploaded.</Item>
  <Item>Relevant UNFPA centralized evaluations uploaded.</Item>
  <Item>Filing status reviewed; outstanding items assigned to specific staff with deadlines.</Item>
  <Item>Access to the repository granted to the evaluation team (read access) before the design phase induction.</Item>
</Checklist>

<KeyTerms>
  <Term name="Online Document Repository">Cloud-based collection of all documents relevant to the CPE, structured by folder and tracked by a checklist.</Term>
  <Term name="CPD">Country Programme Document — the master document approved by the UNFPA Executive Board defining the country programme cycle.</Term>
  <Term name="COAR">Country Office Annual Report — the annual self-reported progress against AWP outputs and the contribution to outcomes.</Term>
  <Term name="DHS / MICS">Demographic and Health Survey / Multiple Indicator Cluster Survey — major periodic household surveys used to triangulate UNFPA outcome data.</Term>
</KeyTerms>
