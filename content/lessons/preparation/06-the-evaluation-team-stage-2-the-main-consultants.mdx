<Callout variant="key" title="A transparent, competitive process — always">
Whether contracting a professional consultancy firm or recruiting individual consultants, the selection must be conducted in a **transparent and competitive manner**. The CPE ToR (with annexes A and D) is attached to the call for consultants.
</Callout>

<TeamRecruitmentFlow />

<h3>Team composition</h3>

<KVGrid>
  <KV title="Typical composition">An international or regional team leader plus two national team members with experience in one or more thematic areas of the UNFPA strategic plan.</KV>

  <KV title="All-national option">If the panel identifies a national consultant with the credentials and no conflict of interest to take the team leader role, the CO may consider a team composed of nationals only.</KV>

  <KV title="Multi-disciplinary requirement">Together, the team must demonstrate the skill mix to address the UNFPA thematic areas relevant to the country programme — typically a combination drawn from SRHR, GEWE, adolescents and youth, and population dynamics.</KV>

  <KV title="Humanitarian expertise">In countries facing humanitarian situations, at least one consultant should have expertise in this area, with relevant in-country humanitarian experience and understanding of the humanitarian reform architecture.</KV>

  <KV title="Methodology requirements">Collectively — and notably the team leader — the team should have experience in evaluation methodology, including gender and human-rights responsive evaluations. Proven experience in qualitative and quantitative data collection. Strong analytic, writing, and presentation skills in the report language.</KV>

  <KV title="Team leader specifically">Able to support the team to implement a sound theory-based approach. Must demonstrate a command of mixed-method approaches to identify the contribution UNFPA interventions have made to a set of changes at outcome level.</KV>
</KVGrid>

<h3>Conflict-of-interest rules</h3>

<Callout variant="warn" title="Who cannot be recruited">
All evaluators must be free of any actual or perceived conflict of interest:
- **Any person who has participated in the design or implementation of the country programme** under evaluation (actual COI).
- **Ex-UNFPA staff from the CO** (real or perceived COI).
- **Current government employees** (perceived or actual COI).

Consultants who have participated in monitoring and/or evaluation work related to the country programme **can** be considered.
</Callout>

<h3>The seven-step selection process (Box 2 of the handbook)</h3>

<Checklist storageKey="ch1-l6-team-stage2-process" title="Box 2 — Process for pre-selection, pre-qualification, selection and recruitment">
  <Item>Publish call for evaluation consultancy with the CPE ToR (annexes A and D only).</Item>
  <Item>Invite suitable applicants to apply (from the pre-qualified CPE consultant directory, UNFPA consultant roster, UN resident coordinator office rosters, RO M&E Adviser referrals, or IEO-managed evaluation teams).</Item>
  <Item>Place applications on the online document repository and alert the assessment panel.</Item>
  <Item>After close of the call, instruct the panel on how to assess consultant CVs against the consultant pre-selection scorecard.</Item>
  <Item>Share completed scorecards and CVs with the RO M&E Adviser for input and pre-qualification.</Item>
  <Item>Conduct interviews of candidates pre-qualified by the RO before making the final decision.</Item>
  <Item>Initiate recruitment with CO operations staff in full compliance with the UNFPA Policy and Procedures for Contracting Individual Consultants.</Item>
</Checklist>

<Callout variant="tip" title="Panel composition">
The selection panel must include **at least 3 people**, with at least one external member (e.g. M&E staff from the government partner coordinating the country programme, or an M&E officer serving in another UN agency).
</Callout>

<h3>Mandatory pre-assignment training</h3>

Prior to starting the design phase, the CPE Manager must ensure that **each consultant**:

<KVGrid>
  <KV title="UNFPA e-learning">Takes the UNFPA e-learning course on country programme evaluations.</KV>

  <KV title="UN BSAFE">Takes the UN e-learning course BSAFE (security awareness).</KV>

  <KV title="PSEA">Takes the UN e-learning on Prevention of Sexual Exploitation and Abuse.</KV>

  <KV title="Logistical readiness">The CO issues a facilitation letter for visa (if needed), provides accommodation assistance, prepares electronic name badges, and schedules a security briefing in line with mandatory UNDSS requirements.</KV>
</KVGrid>

<h3>Recruitment flow at a glance (Figure 4)</h3>

<KVGrid>
  <KV title="Parallel track A — YEE">CPE launch meeting → CPE Manager drafts YEE ToR → Call for YEE consultancy → Panel selection and interviews → YEE recruited and supports CPE Manager during preparation.</KV>

  <KV title="Parallel track B — Main team">Call for evaluation team consultancy → Panel pre-selection → RO pre-qualification → Consultant interviews → Evaluation team recruited → Design phase induction meeting.</KV>
</KVGrid>

<InPractice country="From section 1.6">
The Policies and Procedures Manual (PPM) is a "living document" — the CPE Manager is required to check the PPM for the most updated policy on contracting individual consultants at the inception of every evaluation process. What was acceptable in last year's CPE may no longer be.
</InPractice>

<KeyTerms>
  <Term name="Pre-selection">Initial CV-based filtering against the pre-selection scorecard, performed by the assessment panel.</Term>
  <Term name="Pre-qualification">RO M&E Adviser review of the panel's shortlist; the RO must endorse before the panel proceeds to interviews.</Term>
  <Term name="Conflict of interest">Any condition that compromises an evaluator's independent judgement — actual (participation in programme design/implementation) or perceived (ex-staff, current government employment).</Term>
  <Term name="BSAFE">UN mandatory security awareness e-learning required for all UN personnel before fieldwork.</Term>
  <Term name="PSEA">Prevention of Sexual Exploitation and Abuse — mandatory training for all UN consultants.</Term>
</KeyTerms>
