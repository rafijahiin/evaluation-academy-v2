<p>The CPE Manager is responsible for ensuring that all logistical and practical arrangements for data collection are in place before the team enters the field. The handbook's <strong>Box 10: CPE Manager's checklist for field work preparation</strong> is the reference.</p>

<h3>The logistics checklist</h3>

<KVGrid>
  <KV title="Travel">Transportation and travel authorizations for the team — checked against CO Operations requirements.</KV>

  <KV title="Accommodation">Booked in advance for all sites.</KV>

  <KV title="Security">Further security briefing if needed; BSAFE certification in place for all team members.</KV>

  <KV title="Stipends">Provisions for refreshments and travel allowances for interviewees — especially important in remote areas.</KV>

  <KV title="Government authorisations">Permits for field visits at national and regional levels; courtesy visits arranged.</KV>

  <KV title="Accompaniment">A UNFPA staff member or government counterpart may accompany the team for introductions — but never participates in data collection itself (to avoid bias).</KV>

  <KV title="Interpreters">Selected for SRHR/GEWE/PD/MH expertise and gender-sensitivity (e.g., same-gender interpreters when needed for interviews with women).</KV>

  <KV title="Scheduling">Meetings with all key informants scheduled in advance via a letter from the CO Representative.</KV>

  <KV title="Spaces">Appropriate venues for KIIs (private, neutral) and FGDs (secured, with refreshments).</KV>

  <KV title="Travel claims">Travel authorisations completed; on return, the team submits travel expense claims with supporting documents.</KV>

</KVGrid>

<Callout variant="warn" title="The no-accompaniment rule">
No UNFPA staff — including field-based staff and drivers — are allowed to participate in or attend data collection. This is to minimise the impact of bias on informants. The CPE Manager may participate only when their presence will not affect outcomes (e.g., it can be appropriate at certain debriefing-style meetings, never at rights-holder focus groups).
</Callout>
