<Callout variant="key" title="The last check before fieldwork begins">
At the start of the field phase, the CPE Manager brings the evaluation team — including the YEE — together to discuss the agenda for the field phase and expected achievements. This is the team leader's opportunity to confirm all members are on the right track, and to give space for concerns or requests for clarification.
</Callout>

Select programme staff and field-based staff may participate on an ad-hoc basis where their presence helps unblock specific issues — but the meeting itself is the evaluation team's preparation huddle.

<h3>Box 11 — Checklist for the evaluation team kick-off</h3>

<Checklist storageKey="ch3-l2-kickoff" title="Box 11 — Kick-off meeting checklist">
  <Item>Roles and individual (as well as collective) responsibilities within the team during the field phase are clarified.</Item>
  <Item>Review of field agendas and identification of outstanding issues to be raised with the CPE Manager.</Item>
  <Item>Final check of data collection tools and processes.</Item>
  <Item>Guidance on note-taking and conducting individual and group interviews, focus group discussions, surveys, and on-site observations.</Item>
  <Item>Review of the evaluation matrix: data logging conventions; identification of emerging themes and issues.</Item>
  <Item>Communication channels within the team, with the CPE Manager, and with people assisting the team during fieldwork.</Item>
  <Item>Indicative dates set for team meetings during the field work.</Item>
</Checklist>

<InPractice country="The cadence question">
The handbook expects team meetings during data collection to take place **at least every other day** (see lesson 3.3 on the evaluation matrix). The kick-off is where the team locks in those slots — otherwise the meetings get squeezed out by interview overruns and travel logistics.
</InPractice>

<KeyTerms>
  <Term name="Kick-off meeting">A short team-internal meeting at the start of the field phase to confirm roles, tools, communication, and meeting cadence.</Term>
  <Term name="Field agenda">The week-by-week schedule of interviews and site visits, finalised at the end of the design phase (lesson 2.3).</Term>
  <Term name="Data logging">The convention by which each team member records primary and secondary data into the evaluation matrix — categories, attribution, source tracking.</Term>
</KeyTerms>
