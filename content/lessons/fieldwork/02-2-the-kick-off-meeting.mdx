<p>The team's <strong>kick-off meeting</strong> happens just before data collection begins. The CPE Manager calls it and the RO M&E Adviser participates. The handbook's <strong>Box 11: Checklist for the evaluation team meeting</strong> guides the agenda.</p>

<h3>What the kick-off meeting covers</h3>

<KVGrid>
  <KV title="Review of the agenda">The team confirms the field phase agenda and any last-minute changes.</KV>

  <KV title="Roles in data collection">Who interviews whom; who leads each FGD; who observes; who takes notes.</KV>

  <KV title="The evaluation matrix">Each team member knows which questions and assumptions they own.</KV>

  <KV title="Tools">Interview guides, consent and assent forms, observation checklist, post-interview/follow-up questionnaires.</KV>

  <KV title="Data management">Where files are stored, how recordings are handled, how identifying data is separated.</KV>

  <KV title="Ethical conduct">UNEG ethical guidelines, safeguarding referral pathways, what to do if a disclosure of harm occurs.</KV>

  <KV title="Communication">Daily check-in protocol with the CPE Manager; the every-other-day team meetings.</KV>

</KVGrid>

<h3>Daily rhythm</h3>

<p>The team leader institutes a recurring rhythm:</p>

<ul>
<li><strong>Daily</strong> — short check-ins between team members and with the CPE Manager.</li>
<li><strong>Every other day</strong> — full team meetings to start analysing and interpreting the data being compiled in the matrix. The handbook is specific: these must happen at least every other day during data collection.</li>
<li><strong>Continuously</strong> — entry of data into the evaluation matrix by each team member, organised by assumption.</li>
</ul>
